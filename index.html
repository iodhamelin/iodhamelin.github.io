Hi, I'm Io!

I am a professional illustrator and colorist specialized in character design. I love bringing unique ideas, heroes, and villains to life through my art.

I am especially passionate about creating characters with personality and visual identity, from their expressions and outfits to the colors and small details that make them feel truly unique. I enjoy exploring different moods and styles while always looking for ways to make each illustration tell a story.

Whether you already have a character in mind or just a rough idea you'd like to explore, I enjoy turning those concepts into finished illustrations. Every project is an opportunity to experiment, create, and give something new a visual identity.

If you like my style and have a concept you'd like to bring into reality, send me a message!
